How to read this

  • "2.0× odds" means the bad outcome happened about twice as often after the flag was on as on an average day in the same conditions. It is a tilt in the odds, not a forecast.
  • Every threshold is fixed in advance or measured against the indicator's own past, using only data known on the day.
  • Warnings are often early and sometimes wrong; some crashes come with no warning at all. That is why the radar never changes orders.
  • Data: Cboe (put/call, VXN, VIX, dispersion), FINRA margin debt, multpl (CAPE, CPI inflation), Yahoo (yields, S&P, QQQ, QQEW, BTC), Deribit funding, CFTC futures positioning. Updated after the US close and after the BTC close.
