Daily routine — what to check, and when (UK time)
  1. ~16:05, trading days: open Today. The yellow live-preview box lists any EQQQ order for the Nasdaq trading system or the value plan. Place it in your ISA (and any USD switch in your Invest account) before 16:25.
  2. After you trade: open Journal and record your real fill. The site compares your execution with the system's ideal fills.
  3. ~16:55: the run after the London close confirms or cancels the preview. Nothing to do unless an order was cancelled.
  4. ~00:20 (BTC): the BTC system checks the 00:00 UTC close. If the BTC card shows BUY or SELL, trade at the next opportunity (Kraken Pro limit order) and record the fill.
  5. Weekly: glance at Risk radar. The flags never change orders; they tell you how much risk is building and when the bottom signals are live.
  6. Money in: add top-ups on Portfolio; each system invests them by its own rules. Change capital, fees or the start date in Settings.

The site refreshes at ~16:05 UK (preview), ~16:55 (confirmed), ~21:20 (after the US close: zones, radar, bottom signals) and ~00:20 (BTC). If a feed falls behind, the bar at the top says so; press Refresh data or upload a CSV in Settings.
