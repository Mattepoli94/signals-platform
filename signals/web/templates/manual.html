Positive gamma (price above the flip): ranges

  • Long at the put wall: price dips into it, slows, closes back above → buy. Stop a little below the wall.
  • Short or take profit at the call wall: price rallies into it and stalls → sell. Stop a little above.
  • Target: the middle or the biggest magnet strike, where price tends to settle.
  • Avoid chasing breakouts: most fail and get pulled back into the range.

Negative gamma (price below the flip): trends

  • Don't fade the walls. A clean close through the put wall is a short (breakdown) signal; through the call wall, a long (squeeze) signal.
  • Bounce long only on a reclaim: price breaks the put wall, then closes back above it → long, stop under the low.
  • First target on a bounce: the call wall; a move back above the flip means the regime is calming.
  • Widen stops, halve size: daily ranges are larger and gaps more likely.

Best used as confluence with your own support and resistance, for 1–5 day trades. Levels reset after the monthly expiry ({{ GX.near_expiry|d if GX.near_expiry }}), and big news (CPI, Fed, earnings) can override them.
