  • Allocation: manual trading is meant to be about 30% of the whole portfolio (now 34%).

Systems playbook

The conditions for each system. The same lists appear as tick boxes when you log a trade.

System 1 · long

Box system: early entry

Enter early inside a consolidation box after a mark-up, before the next leg up.

  1. Indices trending (not consolidating), or indices reclaiming the 9-week MA after a flush and a breadth washout
  2. A previous mark-up, then a consolidation before the next leg up
  3. No more than 1 push so far: not 2 higher highs and higher lows on the monthly chart (wicks count), as moves usually come in 2 pushes
  4. A fake breakdown (liquidity grab) raises the odds
  5. A leader: one of the strongest names in one of the strongest sectors
  6. Fundamentals add confluence: earnings, forecasts, profit, balance sheet, insider buying
  7. Above all the MAs, with the MAs curling up
  8. Squeeze Pro on the weekly shows a red or yellow (consolidation) squeeze
  9. Variation B: an undercut of the box that closes back inside on strong volume is a valid early entry
System 2 · long

Breakout trading

Buy the break of the box when the market is trending; get out fast if the break fails.

  1. Indices trending and not chopping
  2. A leader with strong fundamentals
  3. Stop at the first resistance or MA inside the box
  4. The break has momentum and follow-through
  5. An indecision candle or a failed breakout usually sends price back to the origin of the move: exit quickly
System 3 · long

Mean reversion

Buy the first retest of an extreme value zone, with a tight stop, targeting a 50% retracement.

  1. Volatile stock, preferably with strong fundamentals
  2. Levels and value zones marked on the 1-hour or daily chart
  3. Early entry at an imbalance block in the extreme zone (value-area high or low)
  4. First retest only, closing back within the swing low: that is where the reaction is strongest
  5. Tight stop: 1.2× beyond the swing-low wick
  6. Add to the position on a break of structure on a smaller timeframe
  7. Target a 50% retracement or a key level (POC, MAs, resistance)
System 4 · long

Trend following: long

Ride an uptrend; enter on a pullback to a moving average.

  1. Uptrend in place; stop on a close below the slow MA or a market-structure break (MSB)
  2. Low-risk entry on a retest of the fast or slow MA
System 5 · short

Trend following: short

Ride a downtrend; enter short on a rally to a moving average.

  1. Downtrend in place; stop on a close above the slow MA or a market-structure break (MSB)
  2. Low-risk entry on a retest of the fast or slow MA
